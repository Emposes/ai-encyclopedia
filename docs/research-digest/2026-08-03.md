## Proposed (1 issue + 1 stub)

- **Global Workspace / Jacobian-Lens Interpretability** — section addition
  to `chapters/13-mechanistic-interpretability` §5 (Circuits, motifs &
  causal methods). Anthropic's July 6 paper ("Verbalizable Representations
  Form a Global Workspace in Language Models") introduces a genuinely new
  causal instrument (the Jacobian lens) distinct from SAE dictionary
  learning or ablation-based circuit tracing, and an empirical finding — a
  small mid-layer subspace gating verbalizable/controllable behavior — that
  research-scout watched-and-declined on both 2026-07-20 and 2026-07-27
  pending outside replication. That replication has now arrived: an
  independent, non-Anthropic team reproduced the core phenomenon and one
  causal edit on a different open-weight model (Qwen 3.6 27B), though two
  sub-experiments did not replicate cleanly. Mixed-but-real independent
  replication is what moves this from watch-item to proposal. Framed to
  hold the "global workspace"/consciousness press framing at arm's length
  per the chapter's own honest-limits voice. Issue
  [#27](https://github.com/Emposes/ai-encyclopedia/issues/27) · stub:
  `docs/research-queue/global-workspace-jacobian-lens.md`.
