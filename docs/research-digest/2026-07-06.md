## What's new this week

### DeepMind "AI Control Roadmap" — note for the pending AI Safety proposal (#21)

Published 2026-06-18 (deepmind.google/blog/securing-the-future-of-ai-agents/), just before
last week's cutoff, but not previously logged. A defense-in-depth framework treating deployed
agents as insider threats: MITRE ATT&CK-style threat modeling, a Detection/Prevention-Response
tier system, and empirical analysis of 1M coding-agent trajectories (most flagged events are
misinterpretation/overeagerness, not adversarial intent). Substantive and non-marketing, but
it is "control" as a complement to "alignment" — a known distinction in the safety literature,
not a new technique class of its own.

**Coverage check:** Overlaps issue #21 (AI Safety & Scalable Oversight, still open, not yet
authored). → **Not a new proposal.** Note added here so whoever eventually writes #21 folds
this in as the AI-control case study/citation.

### Mistral Leanstral 1.5 — reinforces AI for Science (#22), not standalone

Apache-2.0, 6B-active-param model specialized for Lean 4 formal proof engineering: saturates
miniF2F, solves 587/672 PutnamBench, SOTA on FATE-H/FATE-X (mistral.ai/news/). Genuinely
strong result and open-weight, but it's a specialized capability niche (formal theorem
proving) rather than a general encyclopedia topic on its own.

**Coverage check:** Adjacent to issue #22 (AI for Science) and to the inference-time-compute
chapter (`chapters/12`). → **Not proposed.** See "formal theorem proving" note below — this is
the third independent signal for that thread this year.

### Formal theorem proving / autonomous math research — upgraded from dismissed to monitoring

2026-06-29's digest (and 2026-06-15's) dismissed "Goedel-Architect" formal theorem proving as
"too niche" for a standalone chapter. This week's sweep turned up two more independent data
points: OpenAI's autonomous disproof of the Erdős unit-distance conjecture and DeepMind's
AlphaProof Nexus solving 9 Erdős problems (both **2026-05-20 to 05-26** — six weeks old,
apparently missed by the first sweep's wide lookback window; flagged here for the record
rather than re-litigated). Combined with Leanstral 1.5 this week, that's three separate labs
(OpenAI, DeepMind, Mistral) shipping results in autonomous/formal mathematical reasoning
inside six weeks.

**Disposition:** Still **not proposing a chapter** — this remains a specialized capability
area, and the encyclopedia has no clear track it fits cleanly (closest are `chapters/12`
inference-time compute and the AI-for-Science proposal, neither a strong fit for formal
theorem proving specifically). But three independent labs in six weeks is enough to move this
from "dismissed" to **actively monitored**: if one more corroborating signal (a survey paper,
a fourth lab, or clear production use beyond competition benchmarks) appears in the next few
sweeps, it should be reconsidered for a proposal, possibly as a section within an existing
chapter rather than standalone.

### Conformal calibration for financial tail risk — factual-update candidate, not a chapter

A ~5-month cluster of papers (arXiv:2602.03903, 2603.22569, 2606.18199) applies conformal
prediction to VaR/CVaR estimation under regime shift. `timeseries/06-forecasting-practice.html`
already teaches conformal prediction generically; `quant/06-risk-measurement.html` covers
VaR/CVaR but doesn't mention conformal calibration as a modern nonparametric alternative to
parametric VaR backtesting.

**Coverage check:** Already covered by adjacent chapters; this is a one-paragraph addition, not
a new chapter. **Not a research-scout proposal** — flagging as a candidate for the
feedback-triage auto-fix tier or a future small PR, not acting on it here (research-scout is
propose-only for new content and this doesn't rise to "new chapter or section stub").
