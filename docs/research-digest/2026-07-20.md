## Proposed (3 issues + 3 stubs)

- **State Space Models & Hybrid Architectures** — new chapter, `dl/08`.
  S4 → Mamba → Mamba-2, then the four-lab convergence on hybrid
  Mamba/attention architectures (Jamba, Zamba, NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra,
  Arcee's Trinity Large). No SSM coverage exists anywhere in the encyclopedia
  today. Issue [#23](https://github.com/Emposes/ai-encyclopedia/issues/23) ·
  stub: `docs/research-queue/state-space-models-hybrid-architectures.md`.
- **KV-Cache Compression & Quantization** — section addition to
  `chapters/07-compression`. Eviction (H2O), windowing (StreamingLLM), and
  quantization (KIVI, TurboQuant) as the "other half" of inference memory
  compression, distinct from weight quantization. Issue
  [#24](https://github.com/Emposes/ai-encyclopedia/issues/24) · stub:
  `docs/research-queue/kv-cache-compression.md`.
- **Gradient-Routed Capability Isolation (GRAM)** — section addition to
  `openmodels/05-red-teaming`. Anthropic/AE Studio's July 2026 mechanism for
  making capability removal structural rather than relying on reversible
  post-hoc unlearning. Flagged explicitly as preliminary (one week old,
  small-scale). Issue [#25](https://github.com/Emposes/ai-encyclopedia/issues/25)
  · stub: `docs/research-queue/gradient-routed-capability-isolation.md`.

  `agents/04-harness-engineering`. [openai.com/index/gpt-5-6](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/)

  novelty beyond a pattern other labs already ship. Frontier-roundup update
  only. [x.ai/news](https://x.ai/news)

  Apache-2.0, natively multimodal (text/image/audio/video) with an unusual
  tokenization scheme; largest US open-weight release to date. Candidate for
  an `openmodels` case-study update, but didn't clear the bar for a standalone
  proposal this cycle given three stronger candidates already filed.
  [thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/introducing-inkling/)
